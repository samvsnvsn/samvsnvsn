# Arteloux Optimization — Commercial Hypothesis

Everything here is a hypothesis to be tested, not a decision.

## Model: optimization as a service

Do not assume traditional software licensing. The preferred concept is
optimization as a service: the customer pays for a useful optimized result, not
for software.

Central proposition:

> Do not buy expensive nesting software if you only need the optimized result.

We sell material savings and optimized output rather than a complex CAD/CAM
application.

## Customer journey (hypothesis)

```
UPLOAD PARTS
  → ARTELOUX ENGINE OPTIMIZES
  → FREE OR LOW-FRICTION PREVIEW
  → SHOW UTILIZATION / WASTE / POTENTIAL SAVING
  → CUSTOMER PAYS FOR USEFUL OUTPUT
  → DOWNLOAD FILES
```

## Proof-before-purchase

Where practical, show value before asking for payment.

Example: the customer uploads parts and Arteloux reports

```
Parts:                84
Sheets required:      3
Material utilization: 89.7%
Waste:                10.3%
```

together with the optimized preview. Production-quality, downloadable output is
then monetized.

The exact anti-abuse mechanism (preventing free preview from substituting for
paid output) is not decided yet. See RESEARCH-QUESTIONS.md.

## Possible future commercial models

- pay per optimization
- pay per production package
- credits
- subscription for frequent users
- business/API plans

**Do not set final pricing yet.** Pricing requires market research.

## Target customers

Small businesses, workshops, makers and manufacturers that cut physical
materials and do not need, or cannot justify, a large industrial CAD/CAM suite.
Underserved segments are to be identified by research, not assumed.

## Customer acquisition (future)

The existing Earning Automaton may later become the acquisition and
communication layer. Potential flow:

```
PUBLIC WEB / AGENT REACH / OTHER LEGITIMATE SOURCES
  → LEAD DISCOVERY
  → QUALIFICATION
  → PERMITTED TARGETED OUTREACH
  → ARTELOUX WEBSITE
  → CUSTOMER UPLOAD
  → ARTELOUX ENGINE
  → RESULT
  → PAYMENT
  → DELIVERY
```

Constraints:

- Do not build spam infrastructure.
- The agent should identify genuinely relevant prospects or explicit requests
  and direct interested customers to the product.
- Outreach must be permitted and targeted.
- AgentMail may later handle customer communication.

## Competitive position

Do not compete feature-for-feature with SigmaNEST, ProNest, Lantek and similar
suites. Solve a much simpler problem exceptionally well for customers those
products are too heavy or too expensive for.
